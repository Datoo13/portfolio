About DesktopToWork

Remote work made simple — since 2011

Founded in 2011 and based in The Hague, Netherlands, DesktopToWork is an IT services company specialising in online and digital workspaces. It provides customers with solutions ranging from VOIP, online workspace hosting, and security to comprehensive managed IT services.

With the motto "Work better, live better", DesktopToWork is committed to enabling productive, safe, and simple online working environments for thousands of customers in both small and large businesses. When the demand for remote work surged globally, DesktopToWork needed a storage infrastructure that could scale with it.
