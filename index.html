#KanenasMonos — Christmas Brand Campaign
December 2021 · Greece · Multi-channel
A deeply emotional brand awareness campaign built around Lenovo Greece's Christmas concept — "No One Alone" — using technology as a connector between people separated by distance. Lenovo Greece Always-On · Brand Awareness
No.1 Market in a Global Campaign
2025 — 2026 · Greece · Football Tournament Cycle
During a major global football-tournament promotional cycle, Lenovo ran coordinated social media activity across its markets worldwide. Greece finished first — not through a larger budget, but through a structured operating model applied consistently to every piece of content.

The approach covered full localisation of global assets, paid amplification on every post, warm-audience prioritisation, custom audience compounding, and post-by-post optimisation throughout the campaign. The blended CPM across the programme came in at €1.34 against an industry benchmark of €6–10 — delivering up to 6× the reach per euro spent. Lenovo’s worldwide marketing organisation subsequently invited the team to present the methodology to the broader organisation as a replicable standard.
